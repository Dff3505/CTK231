Program Requirements

The School of Creative Technologies offers several different sequences for students interested in creative technology.

Audio and Music Production

The Audio and Music Production sequence focuses on audio production, recording, sound design, music production, and related technologies. Students in this sequence must maintain an overall cumulative GPA of 2.50.

Interdisciplinary Technologies

The Interdisciplinary Technologies sequence allows students to explore different areas of creative technology and combine multiple interests within the program. This sequence also requires an overall cumulative GPA of 2.50.

Game Design

The Game Design sequence focuses on the creation and development of interactive games and experiences.
