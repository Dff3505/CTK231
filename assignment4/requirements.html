Audio and Music Production

Students interested in Audio and Music Production can study topics related to sound recording, music production, audio technology, and electronic music. Students develop technical and creative skills while working with different types of audio equipment and software. The Audio and Music Production sequence requires an overall cumulative GPA of 2.50.

Interdisciplinary Technologies

The Interdisciplinary Technologies sequence allows students to explore multiple areas of creative technology and combine different skills from the program.

Game Design

The Game Design sequence focuses on the creation and development of digital games.
